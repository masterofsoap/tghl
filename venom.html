About Colorado Venom

Colorado Venom is a competitive hockey organization rooted in Arvada and Lakewood, Colorado. Founded in 2026, we bring together athletes who share a love for the game and a commitment to making a difference.

From ice to inline, every tournament is an opportunity to compete with integrity, build friendships, and support charitable causes across the state. Our home rink is the Foothills Fieldhouse in Lakewood.

🎯 Our Mission: We use the platform of competitive hockey to raise awareness and funds for charitable organizations throughout Colorado, while creating compelling content that showcases the heart of our community.

Beyond the rink, we're a content team dedicated to documenting our journey, sharing stories of resilience, and inspiring the next generation of hockey players who want to make an impact both on and off the ice.
