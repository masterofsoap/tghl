About Colorado Venom

Colorado Venom is based in Arvada and Lakewood, Colorado. The organization was founded in 2026 around the idea that competitive hockey can make a positive impact. Whether competing on ice or inline, every tournament represents an opportunity to compete with integrity, build lifelong friendships, and support charitable initiatives throughout Colorado and beyond.

Our home rink is the Foothills Fieldhouse in Lakewood, a premier indoor sports facility that hosts roller hockey, lacrosse, and more.

Our Teams

Three programs, one mission: compete with purpose and represent Colorado.

Ice Team

Colorado Venom Ice competes in adult hockey tournaments throughout Colorado and neighboring states, emphasizing teamwork, sportsmanship, and representing the organization at a high level while supporting charitable initiatives.

Roller Team

Colorado Venom Roller competes in high-level inline hockey tournaments across the region. Built around speed, puck movement, and relentless effort, the team proudly represents Colorado while helping raise awareness and funding for charitable causes.

Elite Team

Colorado Venom Elite brings together top-level players for premier tournament competition. The Elite roster represents the highest level of Colorado Venom hockey while continuing the organization's commitment to leadership, sportsmanship, and charitable outreach.
